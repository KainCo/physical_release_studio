Turn your GOG offline installers into real, auto-starting backup discs: the tool builds the launcher, autorun files and cover banner for you – ready to burn.

Steam Disc Creator

Split a game folder across CDs, DVDs, Blu-rays or USB sticks. A small Windows installer puts it back together, adds an uninstaller and can also install the required runtimes. Copy-protection hints are created locally from the file names – without any database.

  • Splits across several discs
  • Runs locally in your browser, no uploads
  • 6 installer languages
