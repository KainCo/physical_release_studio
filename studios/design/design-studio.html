USK: the age mark belongs bottom-left on the front. The 34.6 mm default comes from a web summary of the USK guideline and is not verified – please check the current “Guidelines for USK-labels” on usk.de.

Project

Formats: DVD / Blu-ray case = back | spine | front in one piece; jewel case = front booklet and back tray (with its two spines) side by side – cut them apart. Pick “Custom size” for any other case, and check the fold lines with a test print. Icons and texts are editable on the left; in the SVG every part is its own group (feature-pills, store-badges, info-boxes, …).
